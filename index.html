FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer free quotes? +

Yes. Call 065 107 5687 or send us a WhatsApp message with a few details about your project and we will arrange a free, no-obligation quote.

Which areas do you work in? +

Lee Construction serves clients across the Western Cape, South Africa. Get in touch and we will confirm availability in your area.

Do you handle both residential and commercial projects? +

Yes. We build and renovate homes, and we also take on commercial and light-industrial building work.

How long does a renovation or build take? +

It depends on the size and complexity of the job. After the site visit we give you a clear scope, price and realistic timeline in writing.

Can you help with plans and approvals? +

We can guide you through the process and work with your architect or draughtsperson so plans and municipal approvals are in order before building starts.
